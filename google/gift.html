How to verify a contractor in Massachusetts. We already check every box.

  1. Ask for the HIC number. Ours is HIC #212869, active and public on the state HIC lookup. Check the name and status in two minutes.
  2. Check the Construction Supervisor. Our structural and permitted work is supervised under CSL CS-108151, and that license is the one on your permit.
  3. Get the insurance certificate. We carry General Liability up to $1M and Workers' Compensation, and our insurance agent sends the Certificate of Insurance straight to you before we start.
  4. Read the contract. Every Innov project starts with a written contract under Massachusetts law (Chapter 142A), with our HIC number, start and end dates and a deposit of no more than one third of the price.

Innov Builders began with a carpenter's hands and an immigrant's ambition. Questions

Before you claim your gift

What does "terms apply" mean?

The gift is valid for complete kitchen remodels signed Nov 16 to Dec 15, 2026, one gift per project, Massachusetts projects only. Countertop-only, cabinet-only or appliance jobs are not included. The power tower is installed in your new counter at no cost. Gift has no cash value.

What is a pop-up power tower?

Outlets and a wireless charging pad that sit flush inside your countertop and pop up when you need them. Clean counter, phone always charged.

Do I need to start before Dec 15?

No. Sign by Dec 15 and we schedule the start with you.

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

It depends on the scope. You get the full timeline in writing with your estimate.

Which areas do you serve?

Homeowners across Massachusetts, including Wakefield, Reading, North Reading, Lynnfield, Woburn and North Boston.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. Free in-home visit, detailed written quote, no obligation.

Is Innov Builders licensed in Massachusetts?

Yes. Innov Builders is a registered Massachusetts Home Improvement Contractor, HIC #212869, and structural work is supervised by Eduardo Muniz, licensed Construction Supervisor (CSL CS-108151) of our partner company Boston Best Construction (BBC).

Is Innov Builders insured?

Yes. We carry General Liability insurance and Workers' Compensation for our whole crew. A Certificate of Insurance is available on request before your project starts.

What is the difference between an HIC and a CSL?

The HIC registration covers home improvement work on existing owner occupied homes. The CSL is the license required to supervise structural work and pull building permits. Many remodels need both.

Do you pull the building permits?

Yes. When a project requires a permit, we pull it and coordinate every inspection with your town, so you never have to deal with the building department alone.

Can I see your Certificate of Insurance before signing?

Of course. Ask during your free estimate and we will have it sent to you, so you can confirm the coverage before any work begins.
